Kaleido Privacy

Kaleido stores saved website themes, color combos, and picked page areas in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not send this information, website content, or browsing history to a server.

When you first save a site theme, Kaleido asks for optional access to all websites. That permission lets Kaleido work on websites you choose without asking for each domain. Kaleido only changes areas you explicitly pick on a site with an enabled theme. You can revoke the permission in the browser's extension settings.

Kaleido reads the styles of the page elements you pick so it can apply your chosen colors. It does not collect page content, account information, analytics, or advertising data.

You can remove a saved site theme or color combo in the Kaleido popup. Removing Kaleido deletes its local settings.

Limited Use: Kaleido uses site access only to provide the website color theming feature requested by the user. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
