Kaleido Privacy

Kaleido stores your saved colors and the page areas you choose in the browser's local extension storage. It does not send this information, website content, or browsing history to a server.

When you first pick an area, Kaleido asks for optional access to websites so it can apply the colors you save. Kaleido changes only the areas you explicitly pick. You can revoke website access in your browser's extension settings.

Kaleido reads the page element you select so it can apply your chosen color. It does not collect page content, account information, analytics, or advertising data.

You can remove saved colors and picked areas in the Kaleido popup. Removing Kaleido deletes its local settings.

Limited Use: Kaleido uses website access only to provide the manual website-coloring feature requested by the user. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
